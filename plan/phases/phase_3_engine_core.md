## Outcome

fixed-dice test; the label leg is green; a scripted fight resolves end to
end from creation output to a terminal state.

## Scope, in shipping order

Six slices, each its own folder under `packages/engine/src/`, each with a
colocated `*.test.ts` and a `behaviours.ts` registered in `index.ts`.

### 1. `checks/` — the two resolution rolls (MH p.22)

- `skillCheck` — threshold = SKILL + one relevant Martial Proficiency;
  2d6 equal or lower succeeds (R20).
- `luckCheck` — threshold = LUCK; 2d6 equal or lower succeeds; **LUCK -1
  after the check regardless of outcome** (R21).
- Sealed rules (`spec.md`): a **double six fails every check** whatever
  the threshold; **doubles rolls that are not checks** (Final Blow,
  Unexpected Event trigger) are unaffected, and a double six there lands.
- The SKILL/LUCK classification (R22) is the caller's: the engine offers
  both and never picks.

### 2. `combat/` — the round (MH p.23-29)

- `attackStrength` — 2d6 + SKILL + **exactly one** relevant Martial
  Proficiency (R23, D10). Training does **not** add (I-22).
- `resolveRound` — compare the two Attack Strengths:
  - opponent higher -> Master ENDURANCE minus the difference (R24);
  - Master higher -> the winner's **four** options as a discriminated
    union, not a chosen one: `damage` (the difference), `technique`
    (costs its value in ENDURANCE, no roll — R25b/R27), `weapon`
    (change or recover — R25c), `opening` (no damage — R29). Techniques
    are available **only** as the winner's option in combat (I-23).
  - equal -> `unexpected-event`; **the combat phase ends** (R32).
- `finalBlow` — after an Opening, 2d6; doubles land (R30). A landed blow
  may become a new Technique: roll against current LUCK, **-1 on failure
  only, no decrement on success** (R31, I-12). The naming table
  (`rules/final-blow.json`) is inspiration only — the engine returns the
  three words, never a name.
- A Praying Mantis Master's landed Final Blow is lethal — outcome only,
  the doubles roll is unchanged (R13, I-25).
- `unexpectedEvent` — 2d6 into `rules/unexpected-events.json`; rows
  carrying `retreatRow` roll **Morale** (sealed: 1-3 flee, 4-5 cautious
  retreat, 6 rally +1d6). Rows 2-5 and 9-12 carry I-30's minimum
  mechanical readings as data, labelled `reading`.
- `endsFight` — R26: a landed Final Blow, either ENDURANCE at zero, or an
  Unexpected Event.

### 3. `multiple/` — several opponents (MH p.30)

- `skillForFight` — SKILL minus opponent count (R35).
- `roundAgainstMany` — **one** Master roll; each attacking opponent up to
  its `ATTACK` rolls its own; each comparison resolves independently
  (I-06).
- `attackersThisRound` — `ATTACK` caps simultaneous attackers (R37);
  against a **lone** Master-side fight ATTACK is inert (build-plan row).
  Singular named monsters follow I-05.
- `areaDamage` — the same amount to `reach` enemies (R36), `reach` read
  from the ability's prose (I-11: two / all / all / two).

### 4. `escape/` — leaving a fight (MH p.30)

- `escape` — without a Technique flagged `escape` or an approved
  stratagem: ENDURANCE -2 and **1 Dishonor Point** (R38, R39, I-32). The
  -2 counts as damage, so a bare escape always dishonors.

### 5. `healing/` — recovery (MH p.31)

- `heal` over `rules/healing.json`: SKILL +1 by spiritual regeneration,
  full on a **night's rest** (sealed); ENDURANCE +4 by healing technique,
  regeneration, a meal or a Health Elixir, full after a **week**; LUCK +1
  by a Temple Spirituality check, no full restore (R40-R42).

### 6. `progression/` — XP, treasure, immunity, the Oracle (MH p.32-33, 42, 58, 66-67)

- `xpAward` — by SKILL band with the printed caps, minus Dishonor (R43+).
- `ordinaryBlowsPass` — spirits are immune to ordinary blows unless the
  weapon is exceptional (R77, I-29 tag list).
- `encounterByColumn` — 2d6 into `rules/encounters.json` by column.
- `treasureRoll` — 1d6 by ENDURANCE band; `specialItem` — 2d6.
- `oracle` — the Oracle procedure's roll into `world/oracle.json`, plus
  I-07a ("Special" = the opponent adds one listed Proficiency) and
  I-08a (Ambush = one unopposed opponent round first).

## Conventions

- No new content records: every table this phase reads already shipped in
  Phase 2. If a table is missing a field the engine needs, add the field
  to the **content** package with its citation, in the same commit.
- `{label, cite}` per behaviour: `rule` cites the folio (`MH p.23`),
  `reading` cites its `I-nn`, `invention` cites the decision.
- Fixed-dice tests throughout via `fromSequence`; no randomness in tests.
- Small files: one concept per file, colocated tests, no dense modules.

## Done when

1. Every rule named above has a fixed-dice test.
2. `npm run labels:check` is green — no unlabelled export.
3. A scripted fight (`combat/fight.test.ts`) runs a preset Master against
   a rostered opponent from first round to a terminal state under a fixed
   dice sequence, asserting the terminal reason.
4. `npm run verify` is green end to end.
