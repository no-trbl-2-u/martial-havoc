## What this repository does with it

- The whole repository is CC BY-SA 4.0 (`../LICENSE`), the licence of
  the works it builds on (`../spec.md`, Routine calls).
- The app ships an attribution screen naming Gianluca Monaco, Cristian
  Cammarata, limofeus and watabou (build plan, Phase 9).
- None of the credited art ships: text and SVG only (`../spec.md`,
  Refusals). The two PDFs at the repository root are the sources as
  published and are never edited.
