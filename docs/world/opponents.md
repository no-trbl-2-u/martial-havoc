# Opponents

Fifty stat blocks, printed alphabetically on pp. 70-79, each as: name, a one-line description, SKILL, ENDURANCE, ATTACK, and Proficiencies with values in parentheses (R80).[^rulebook] Descriptions are verbatim.

How the numbers are read:

- ATTACK "indicates how many enemies can attack at the same time" (p. 30, R37).[^rulebook]
- A named Proficiency's value adds to the opponent's Attack Strength; an opponent printed with "Technique (n)" or "Ritual (n)" adds n likewise and "does not spend ENDURANCE points to perform Techniques or Rituals" (p. 66, R76).[^rulebook]
- "Martial Arts (n)" means: roll a Martial Art on the [Martial Arts](martial-arts.md) table and give each of its Proficiencies n points (p. 66, R75).[^rulebook]
- "Sometimes you will face spirits or ghosts, incorporeal beings immune to traditional weapons or blows; you will need to use a technique, ritual, or exceptional weapon to defeat them" (p. 66, R77). No block is tagged incorporeal by the book.[^rulebook]

Which opponent is met where is the Encounters matrix of p. 67 (Urban, Non-urban, Water, Supernatural, Monastery columns), which lives in [encounters](../rules/encounters.md). 49 of the 50 are reachable from it; the Dealer is reached only from the Gambling House (p. 46, R57).[^inventory]

# Notes

- Five blocks carry a single Proficiency, all of the "Martial Arts (n)" kind: First Abbot (3), Mercenary (2), Shi Di (1), Shi Fu (2), Shi Gong (4). Youxia carries "Martial Arts (2)" beside Code of Chivalry (4).[^inventory]
- ATTACK: 32 of the 49 stated values are 1; Brawler is a range (2-4); Huang Feng Guai is blank.[^inventory]
- ENDURANCE bands for the Treasure table (p. 68, R78; derived, not stated): 20 or more - Boxer, Dapeng, Kobukson, Kun, Long Wang, Rebels, Shi Shi, Youxia (8); 17-19 - Bai Gu Jing, Beast, Feng Huang, Green Hornet, Huang Feng Guai, Mercenary, Niu Mowang, Pan Long, Pirate, Shan Xiao, Shen, Shi Fu, Shi Gong, Xiongu (14); up to 16 - the remaining 28.[^inventory]
- Optional Minions rule (footnote to Unexpected Event 7, p. 28, R33): "you can consider Minions with ENDURANCE=1; if you hit you can remove one minion".[^rulebook]
- Opponent Proficiency names are not the Master's Proficiencies; none of them appears on the [Martial Arts](martial-arts.md) table except by coincidence of wording (Disarm, Escape, Trapping).
- The nine stat blocks of The 5 Treasures (Devil servant, Dexterous Ghost, Junior King Silver Horn, Monk, Ogre, Old Vixen, Senior King Golden Horn, Skillful Beast, Woodgatherer) use the same schema with a LOOT line added and live with the adventure; see [the 5 Treasures readings](../rules/readings/the-5-treasures.md).[^inventory]
