# Presets ship as printed

The eight sheets are the author's, published under CC BY-SA 4.0, and are reproduced here exactly as transcribed. Yin's overspend and Sun Wukong's out-of-range SKILL are recorded, not corrected: a preset is data (R83), not a creation the engine validates, and the validation rules R10, R11, R15 and R16 apply to Masters the player builds. An engine that offers these presets should load them verbatim and may surface the note.

# Notes

- Equipment on the sheets is free text, consistent with R02 ("even if not listed in the weapon table"): Nunchaku, Revolver, Magical sword, Magical staff, Legendary weapon and Wine bottle are not Market items (see [market](market.md)).[^inventory]
- Beggar So's Wine bottle is the alcohol Drunken style requires (R13).
- The "From" column names the films these Masters are inspired by; six of the seven films are on the book's [partial filmography](filmography.md) (Drunken Master, Fist of Fury, Chinese Ghost Story, The 36th Chamber of Shaolin, The Scorpion King and Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon are listed; Come Drink With Me is not; Journey to the West is the novel).
