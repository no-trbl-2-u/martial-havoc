# Table of Deities

Printed on p. 29, immediately after the Unexpected Event table, "For divine intervention or to generate random deities" (R34).[^rulebook] First d6 banded 1-3 / 4-6, second d6 1-6: twelve rows, three columns to combine into a name such as "Great Sage, Destroyer of Prosperity". The inventory notes that this is a banded 12-row table, not a d66 (D02).[^inventory]

# Notes

- "Guardian" appears twice in the Action column (1-3/2 and 1-3/5) and "Destroyer" twice (1-3/4 and 4-6/5), as printed.[^rulebook]
- Where deities otherwise appear: Unexpected Event 2 "Adverse divine intervention" and 12 "Favorable divine intervention" (p. 28); the Temple's "three stars of good fortune - Sanxing" (p. 47); Rituals that invoke a named god (God of Money, God of Plagues, God of Rain, King of the Underworld, Lord of the Bow, Lord of the Sea, Mother of Lightning, Protective Demon, The Eight Celestial Drunkards, White Guanyin; see [Rituals](rituals.md)); the Special Items named for the Eight Immortals (p. 69, see [treasures and special items](../rules/treasures-and-special-items.md)); the opponents Tutelary Spirit ("Emanation of the local deity") and Long Wang ("guardian of the gods"; see [opponents](opponents.md)).[^inventory]
- The book's own examples of divine manifestation: "a deity can manifest itself with flashes of fire or as an invisible hand" (Appendix B, p. 85; see [cinematic journey](cinematic-journey.md)).[^rulebook]
