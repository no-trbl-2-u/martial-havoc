# Where the book cites films elsewhere

- Introduction (p. 3): "inspired by films such as 'Five Fingers of Violence', 'Drunken Master' or 'Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon'". The first appears on the list as Five Fingers of Death (1972).[^rulebook]
- Appendix B worked examples (pp. 88-91): Clan of the White Lotus (1980) and Zu: Warriors from the Magic Mountain (1983); the second is not on the list. See [cinematic journey](cinematic-journey.md).[^rulebook]
- Appendix C "From" column (p. 92): Drunken master (1978), Fist of fury (1972), A chinese ghost story (1987), Come drink with me (1966), The 36th CHamber of Shaolin (1978), Journey to the west (16th century), The scorpion king (1992), Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (2000). Come Drink With Me is not on the list; Journey to the West is the novel. See [pre-generated Masters](pregenerated-masters.md).[^inventory]
