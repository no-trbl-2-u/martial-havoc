# World

`world/` holds what exists in the game: the Martial Arts a Master can practise, the Techniques and Rituals they can learn, the deities, the goods on sale, the opponents, the ready-made Masters, and the word tables (Oracle, Inspirations, Sparks) the player rolls on. It does not say how play works: the procedures of creation, checks, combat, healing, advancement, exploration, cities, encounters, treasures and the Oracle procedure live in `rules/`, and adventures such as The 5 Treasures live in `campaigns/`. Every table here is copied cell for cell from the estate inventory (section 3), checked against the pdf-parse extraction of the rulebook, cited by printed folio (PDF page = folio + 1), and ends with an "Engine notes" section naming the rules rows (R-nn) that consume it and the readings (I-nn) that bear on it. The material is Gianluca Monaco's, CC BY-SA 4.0.

# Styles and powers

* [Martial Arts](martial-arts.md) - The 18 Martial Arts a Master may practise, each with its style text and its Martial Proficiencies, on the banded 1d6 x 1d6 table of pp. 7-10.
* [Techniques](techniques.md) - The 36 Techniques of the d66 Techniques Table (pp. 12-15), each with its printed pinyin, its value (Resource points to learn, ENDURANCE to use) and its verbatim effect.
* [Rituals](rituals.md) - The 36 Rituals of the d66 Rituals Table (pp. 16-19), each with its printed pinyin, its value (Resource points to learn, ENDURANCE to use) and its verbatim effect.

# Beings

* [Table of Deities](deities.md) - The banded 1d6 x 1d6 Table of Deities (p. 29): twelve rows of Name, Action and Object to combine, for divine intervention or to generate random deities.
* [Opponents](opponents.md) - All 50 opponent stat blocks of pp. 70-79 in one alphabetical table: description, SKILL, ENDURANCE, ATTACK, Proficiencies with values, page and source notes.
* [Pre-generated Masters](pregenerated-masters.md) - Appendix C's eight ready-to-play Master sheets (image on p. 92) transcribed field by field, with the arithmetic check that shows Yin overspends and the decision that presets ship as printed.

# Goods

* [Market](market.md) - The four Market price lists (pp. 52-55): Common Items, Weapons, Expedition Equipment and Armor, with the rules the book attaches to each list.

# Word tables

* [Oracle](oracle.md) - The Oracle table of p. 58: eleven 1d6 rows (Closed Question, Outcome, NPC reaction, Creature Reaction, Encounter Outcome, Enemy Type, No. of enemies, Enemy attack, Door, Object amount, Value), every cell, with the inferred column spans marked.
* [Inspirations](inspirations.md) - The two d66 Inspirations tables (pp. 59-60), Action and Theme, 36 words each, combined to answer an open question or to supply a stimulus.
* [Sparks](sparks.md) - The six d66 Spark tables (pp. 60-63), 216 random words of objects, places and themes; roll 1d6 for the table, then d66 for the word.
