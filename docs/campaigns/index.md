`campaigns/` holds adventures: the authored scenes a Master plays through,
and the hooks that start them. Procedures live in `../rules/`; what exists
in the world (opponents, deities, places) lives in `../world/`. The 5
Treasures, Gianluca Monaco's two-page adventure for Martial Havoc, is the
first file of a public adventure format; Phase 5 of
`plan/steps/01_build_plan.md` defines that format, and the concepts here
record the adventure as printed so that the format has one place to cite.

# Hooks

* [Adventure hooks](adventure-hooks.md) - the rulebook's d66 table of 36 adventure hooks, verbatim, with the adventure rules rows (MH p.36-39).

# Adventures

* [The 5 Treasures](the-5-treasures/) - the Lotus Flower cave on the Flat-top mountain: eight areas, nine foes, five treasures; see its [index](the-5-treasures/index.md).
