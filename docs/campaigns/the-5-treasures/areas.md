# 1. Flat-top mountain

> A wild and vast territory covered with pines and willow trees, deep
> valleys and steep rocks, difficult paths for horses. In the distance an
> axe at work and the animals running on craggy ridges.[^adventure]

**Encounters:** 1-3 Woodgatherer; 4-5 Ogre; 6 Junior King.[^adventure]

**Hint:** "Old poem 'Towering peaks, Tapered pinnacles. Down in the deep,
winding stream, Beside the lonely cliff'"[^adventure]

**Gates:** none; the mountain is the approach to the cave.

**Foes and loot:** Woodgatherer (LOOT: Great Bear scripture [recited keeps
beasts away]); Ogre (LOOT: Spear); Junior King Silver Horn (LOOT:
seven-star sword). The Junior King met here is the same Junior King as in
the Dining Hall - one entity in several tables `(inferred, I-33c)`.
Woodgatherers are plural ("They") with ATT 5 `(inferred, I-05b)`: a band
of woodgatherers.[^inventory]

**Day and night:** nothing recorded.

# 2. Cave entrance

> A shut wooden gate hidden by a willow tree; painted in red : 蓮花洞 A
> stream falls slowly silent, swallowed by the mountain. Shrill voices come
> from the inside.[^adventure]

**Encounters:** 1 Devil servant; 2-4 Ogre; 5-6 Junior King.[^adventure]

**Hint:** "Ogres go out hunting for travellers once a day"[^adventure]

**Gates:** the shut wooden gate. No lock is stated. On the map, the arrow
marks the way in; the unmarked chamber inside is the Cave entrance's
inside `(inferred, I-42)`.[^inventory]

**Foes and loot:** Devil servant (LOOT: 1-3 junk; 4-5 simple weapon; 6
[warning-triangle icon, no text]); Ogre (LOOT: Spear); Junior King Silver
Horn (LOOT: seven-star sword; one entity, `(inferred, I-33c)`).

**Day and night:** the Ogres "go out hunting for travellers once a day";
the adventure has a clock and states where the rulebook has none
`(inferred, I-40)`. The Cave entrance's Ogres are absent by night
`(inferred, I-45)`; night is set by opening the gourd
([treasures](treasures.md)).[^inventory]

# 3. Attendants room

> The room serves as entrance to the locked Private Quarters, a kitsch
> display case contains the finest tea set, right next to it, on a pedestal
> a jade vase is guarded by at least one of the personal Senior King's
> attendants.[^adventure]

**Encounters:** 1-2 Skillful Beast; 3-4 Dexterous Ghost; 5-6
Both.[^adventure]

**Hint:** "The attendants have already tested Senior King's patience. Next
mistake they will get punished"[^adventure]

**Gates:** this room is "entrance to the locked Private Quarters". Both
attendants carry "private quarter's key". The Private Quarters are the
Chieftain quarter and the Women quarter, both behind the Attendants room;
one key opens both; two copies exist because either attendant may be met
alone `(inferred, I-07)`.[^inventory]

**Foes and loot:** Skillful Beast (LOOT: private quarter's key); Dexterous
Ghost (LOOT: private quarter's key); "Both" = the two together, a multiple
combat with two opponents (R35).[^inventory]

**Treasure present:** the Vase of muttonfat jade - "on a pedestal a jade
vase is guarded" - is picked up by exploration, not loot `(inferred,
I-38)`.[^inventory]

**Day and night:** nothing recorded.

# 4. Dining Hall

> The cave vault is high and the torches barely illuminate the entire area,
> a long stone table with two stone thrones . The room is busy with devil
> servants running to and from the kitchen.[^adventure]

**Encounters:** 1-4 Devil servant; 5 Junior King; 6 Senior
King.[^adventure]

**Hint:** "Junior King would take a nap here if he overdrank wine."[^adventure]

**Gates:** none.

**Foes and loot:** Devil servant (LOOT: 1-3 junk; 4-5 simple weapon; 6
[warning-triangle icon, no text]; number "use the Oracle"); Junior King
Silver Horn (LOOT: seven-star sword); Senior King Golden Horn (LOOT:
Plantain fan). Defeating the Junior King anywhere removes him everywhere;
his sword drops once `(inferred, I-33c)`.[^inventory]

**Day and night:** the nap is a state: "overdrank" is a player-caused
state (bring wine) `(inferred, I-40)`.[^inventory]

# 5. Storage room

loot `(inferred, I-38)`.[^inventory]

**Day and night:** the gourd, once opened, changes "day to night"; it sets
the day/night flag `(inferred, I-45)`.[^inventory]

# 6. Women quarter

> The door is decorated with ivory bas-reliefs: a rich sedan chair followed
> by a troop of marching she-devils. A protruding hand with a golden

> wrapped around her head and jewels studded her golden earrings. A
> she-devil servant is combing her hair.[^adventure]

**Encounters:** "Old Vixen and she-Devil servant" (no dice).[^adventure]

**Hint:** "The old lady is the demons' adoptive mother. She knows the
spells to control the Dazzling Golden Cord"[^adventure]

**Gates:** the decorated door. Part of the locked Private Quarters, reached
through the Attendants room and its key `(inferred, I-07)`; on the map it
is entered from the Chieftain quarter `(inferred, I-42)`.[^inventory]

**Foes and loot:** Old Vixen (LOOT: Dazzling Golden Cord) and a she-Devil
servant, a fixed encounter; the she-Devil servant uses the Devil servant
block `(inferred, I-34)`. The Cord is inert loot until the spells are
known: from her (Kind/Helpful reaction), or from the Chieftain's sheets
`(inferred, I-41)`.[^inventory]

**Day and night:** nothing recorded.

**Day and night:** nothing recorded.
