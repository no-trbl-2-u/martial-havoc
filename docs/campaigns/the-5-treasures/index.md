Eight areas, nine foes and five treasures in the Lotus Flower cave on the
Flat-top mountain; a gourd that toggles day and night, one private-quarter
key held by both the Dexterous Ghost and the Skillful Beast, the Junior
King as one entity across several encounter tables, and a captive Monk who
is a rescue rather than an enemy.
