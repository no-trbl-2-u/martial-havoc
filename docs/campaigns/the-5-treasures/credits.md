# Notes

  pages; the rulebook's Cristian Cammarata is not credited on the
  adventure.[^inventory]
- The adventure PDF carries no CreationDate or ModDate (a Google Docs
  export with those fields undefined), so no `last_modified` is recorded on
  its source entries.[^inventory]
- The whole of The 5 Treasures is CC BY-SA 4.0 material by Gianluca
  Monaco; the concepts under this directory quote it under that licence
